## ArcMenu v74 / 70.0

ArcMenu had advanced from the previously audited v73 runtime to v74.

The exact v74 EGO archive was audited instead of downgrading the extension. The accepted implementation keeps the upstream catalog and applies only the exact-version binding fix. A temporary candidate-only PowerButton patch was tested during diagnosis and then removed because physical validation showed that the domain binding alone was sufficient.

Physical acceptance included Polish power-button tooltips after logout/login.
